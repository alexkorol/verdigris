Use case: stylized-concept.
Create one square game ground texture: quiet natural packed earth, seen straight down. It is a single homogeneous patch of warm muted brown soil, filling the whole opaque square. Construct it as deliberate 64-by-64 logical pixel art, enlarged with hard square pixel clusters and a restrained palette of eight closely spaced earth-brown tones. Keep almost all of the surface a calm middle brown; add only low-contrast compact grit clusters and a few tiny embedded pebbles. Even, diffuse light and even color distribution all the way to every edge. This ground must remain visually subordinate to small characters standing on it. No cracks, diagonal lines, lattice, paving stones, border, path, grass, large rocks, vignette, directional shadow, text, or scenery. One texture only.

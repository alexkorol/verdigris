# Player-experience / art / audio review prompt

Review the specified packaged Verdigris build, content hash, seed, platform and scenario. This is an evidence-gathering role, not permission to change code or invent measurements.

Use ordinary controls and a fresh disposable profile where required. Do not inject items, mutate simulation state or guide the tester unless the protocol allows it; log every intervention. Capture the beginning, dangerous fight, loot/equip decision, route/return decision and House/death consequence rather than only a staged beauty shot.

Assess each dimension separately: input responsiveness; threat legibility and counterplay; attack weight and timing; distinct build behavior; reward comprehension; navigation; UI hierarchy; material/animation consistency; audible warning/impact/mix; accessibility; persistence after restart. A strong score elsewhere does not cancel an integrity failure or an unreadable lethal threat.

For art, compare normal-camera gameplay with the approved in-game target. Inspect silhouettes, proportions, scale, feet/pivots, collision alignment, lighting/material response, occlusion, animation phases, VFX hierarchy and equipment representation. Verify asset provenance; do not certify external artwork by appearance alone.

For audio, listen to a dense fight and transitions using stated settings. Identify missing, duplicated, late, masked or fatiguing cues. A cue-event trace proves dispatch, not that the sound was audible or useful.

For fresh players, ask after—not before—the decision: What were you trying to do? What hurt you? What could you have done? Why did you keep or equip that item? What happens if the Scion dies? Record actual answers and behavior. Small samples are formative feedback, not population statistics.

Report raw observations, timestamps, build/content/seed, platform/settings, capture hashes and limitations. Turn each failure into one concrete correction with expected behavior. Do not certify a build you did not run, an audio mix you did not hear, or a platform you did not test.

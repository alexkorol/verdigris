## Quality gates by tier

### Vertical slice

One complete owner-playable House-to-extraction-to-recovery journey; coherent
representative combat, skill, monster, item, progression, zone, UI, animation,
VFX, and audio content; deterministic save/replay; keyboard/accessibility
baseline; captured regression and clean-machine launch proof. Placeholder
breadth is allowed only when visibly labeled and outside the demonstrated loop.

### Complete indie ARPG

All campaign/endgame paths complete; every content family has validated authoring
and migration tooling; no debug presentation on default path; difficulty,
economy, performance, accessibility, resolution/device, save recovery, soak,
packaging, update, visual/audio regression, and repeated owner-play gates pass.
Content may reuse production sets deliberately, but not as accidental omission.

### Blockbuster planning envelope

The indie gates plus sustained breadth/variation budgets, multi-stage bosses,
deep build/item/endgame diversity, production animation/VFX/audio/music and
cinematic-equivalent presentation, localization seams, large-scale content QA,
telemetry-informed tuning infrastructure, platform release/signing/notarization
where applicable, upgrade/support plans, and repeated clean-machine/soak/owner
verdicts across representative campaign and endgame journeys.

## Owner-input batching

Quantity, lore, naming, art direction, magic direction, economy, seasons,
weather, music, and final quality/scale choices remain owner decisions. Batch
them as narrow alternatives with affected graph nodes and nonblocking work
listed. Do not block content-neutral schemas, tools, validators, fixtures,
placeholders, test matrices, or unrelated systems while awaiting a ruling.

# OI-006 — House crafting and economy boundary

**State:** PARKED-NONCRITICAL. **Deadline:** before rates, sinks, trade, passive
income, or currency exchange are implemented.

Decision required: select the first House service boundary. Recommended:
durable stores and material-driven crafting roles first, with async trade,
passive income, and exchange held behind explicit caps/safety design.
Alternatives: crafting-only; or a separately prototyped economy sandbox with no
production persistence. Acceptance: House—not hideout bench—ownership, no
genre-default rates, deterministic/offline resolution defined, abuse and sink
model reviewed. Fallback: item lifecycle, extraction, history, and schema work.

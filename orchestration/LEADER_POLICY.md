# Verdigris event-driven leader policy

The portable `alexkorol/orchestration` controller is the intended always-on
supervisor. Codex Sol/Fable are scarce Tier-C authorities and must not spend
turns on polling, queue counting, routine packet prose, ordinary gate reruns,
evidence collation, permission handling, worktree preparation, or mechanical
merge preparation.

## Tier A — mechanical and low risk

Exact deterministic gates, current-base check, protected-path check, and an
independent cheap/free review may satisfy the review contract. Controller
integration is permitted only after an explicit standing policy names eligible
paths, merge target, rollback evidence, and protected-branch preconditions.

## Tier B — bounded design

Requires deterministic gates, two independent reviews from different model
families where available, and a controller evidence bundle. Escalate only on
disagreement, protected-path impact, suspicious evidence, or repeated failure.

## Tier C — architecture and product authority

Requires Sol/Fable or the designated architecture leader. Tier C includes core
interface freezes, wire protocol changes, persistence formats, deterministic
simulation invariants, production dependencies, owner-only product rulings,
and irreversible release/account actions.

The controller emits a compact validated `LEADER_BRIEF` for Tier C or conflict.
It identifies the authority hash, changed SHAs and paths, failing/conflicting
evidence, protected invariants, exact decisions required, requested output, and
maximum scope. Routine state changes do not wake the leader.

Until the orchd vertical slice is accepted, a temporary monitor may perform a
short read-only health check no more than hourly. It must not reason over the
whole repository or perform worker implementation.
